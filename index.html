Love in Lockdown

Our Story

We met online in early 2020, at the very start of the COVID pandemic. After weeks of late-night chats, we finally met in person. With restrictions still in place, our first few months together were filled with simple, meaningful moments, meals in the car, long walks around the park, and conversations that made time fly.

Since then, we've built our lives side by side. We've travelled to 16 countries, bought a home, and cherish the everyday moments as much as the adventures.

In May 2024, during a trip to South Korea, Diego proposed, and we said yes to a lifetime of love, laughter, and partnership.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bring my kids or a plus one?

As this will be a small, intimate celebration, we kindly ask that only the guests named on the invitation attend. Little ones are more than welcome! Please list each attendee's name on the RSVP so we can account for everyone.

Dress Code

Our dress code is formal: gentlemen in suits, ladies in cocktail or long dresses. As our celebration takes place at the end of autumn, the evening may get chilly. We recommend bringing a coat or wrap to stay cosy.

Accommodation

There's no accommodation at the venue itself. The nearest town is Bowral, just 20 minutes away, with plenty of hotels and Airbnbs to choose from. Driving home is also an option as the night won't run too late.

Arrival & Parking

Please arrive no earlier than 2:00 pm for a 2:30 pm ceremony start. The venue is best reached by car as public transport isn't available. There is plenty of free parking onsite, and staff will guide you to the ceremony area.

Gifts

Your presence at our wedding is a gift in itself. However, for those who would like, a wishing well will be available on the day. Your generosity is always appreciated.
